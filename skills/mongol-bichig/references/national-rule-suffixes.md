# What the national orthography rule teaches about suffixes and endings

What this knowledge base learned about suffixes (дагавар) and endings
(нөхцөл) from *Монгол бичгийн зөв бичих дүрэм* (Institute of Language and
Literature, Mongolian Academy of Sciences, 2025), Mongolia's national rule
since 2026-02-11. These are the lessons, stated in our own words. The book is
not reproduced here; read it for its tables and examples. The rest of what it
teaches is in [national-rule.md](national-rule.md), which also lists the
repository owner's rulings where the book and this knowledge base differ.

The book speaks of writing a suffix *attached* (залгаж, inside the word) or
*following* (дагуулж, after the stem and apart from it). It never says which
character does the separating, so the connector-or-space choice stays with
`suffixes.md`.

## What is attached and what follows

- **Word-building suffixes are attached**, almost without exception.
- **Case endings and the possessive and reflexive endings follow the stem.**
  So do the plurals -ууд / -нууд and нар, and the negative үгүй.
- **The plurals -д, -с, -чууд and -чуул are attached.** -с and -чууд drop a
  stem-final н first.
- **All verbal endings are attached**, the habitual -даг among them. A verbal
  ending never stands apart from its stem.
- **The belonging suffix -х / -хан follows only after a genitive**
  (тэднийх, нутгийнхан). Anywhere else it is attached (холхи).
- **Pronouns with a case ending are single words** by convention (надад,
  намайг).
- **-тай** is, for the book, written following as a case ending and attached
  as an adjective-forming or modal suffix (хэрэгтэй, ёстой). This knowledge
  base treats the join as a stylistic choice that formal documents resolve by
  attaching; see the rulings.
- **Particles after a head word follow it**: л, ч, шиг, the question
  particle, бэ / вэ, сан.

## Which form of a suffix

- **Vowel harmony** chooses between a masculine and a feminine written form.
  A Cyrillic suffix with four vowel variants usually has only two written
  forms, and some have one: -лаг is always written `lig`, whatever the word.
- **Plural**: -нууд (`nuγud` / `nügüd`) after a vowel or н; -ууд (`ud` /
  `üd`) after any other consonant.
- **Hard and soft finals.** The hard finals are б, г, р, с, д; the soft ones
  н, м, л, нг, and also й and в. Endings that begin with д / т or ж / ч take
  the voiceless variant after a hard final: the dative is -т after one and -д
  otherwise, and likewise -ч against -ж in the converb and the past.
- **A suffix beginning with a long vowel** (-аа, -аач, -аан, -аад) is written
  with γ/g before its vowel after a stem ending in a vowel, and with y after a
  stem ending in i.
- **γ/g becomes q/k after д or с** in several suffixes, the causative among
  them.
- **A linking vowel u / ü** comes between a consonant-final stem and a suffix
  that cannot follow a consonant directly: the causative -уул, the passive,
  the reciprocal and cooperative, and many verbal endings. Seven stem finals
  call for it (н, б, д, р, с, л, г) before endings that begin with y, γ/g, n,
  ng, m, l or r. Endings beginning with b, s, d, ǰ, č, q/k or t attach
  directly.
- **Stem-final н** is written as ng before γ/g, q/k or n, and is dropped
  before a number of word-building suffixes.

## Numerals

The numeral suffixes are attached to the number word: the approximate -аад,
the ordinals -дугаар, -дахь and -дагч, the collectives -уул and -л, and the
multiplicative -таа. After a digit the ending is written following it with no
punctuation between, and in full, never abbreviated.

## Consistency within one text

The book advises, as guidance and not as a rule, against mixing two things in
a single text:

- **Older and modernised endings.** The older set is the dative `tur` /
  `dur`, the third-person possessive `anu` / `inü`, and the long verbal
  endings; the modernised set is `tu` / `du`, `ni`, and the short ones. Use
  one set throughout.
- **Separated and fused case + reflexive forms.** `tu-ban` beside `taγan`,
  `eče-ben` beside `ečegen`, `tai-ban` beside `taiγan`: choose one way.

## What it confirmed in the registry

Every attachment condition `suffixes.md` already stated agrees with the book,
including the hard-final set and the directive as one invariant word. It also
confirmed the fused forms the registry had marked as expected but
unregistered, and supplied the reasons for today's changes there: the plural
after н, the fused -тайгаа, and the removal of -даг from the
connector-joined entries.
