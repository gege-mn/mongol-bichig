## The four principles (p.8)

1. **Авиа зүйн зарчим** — write close to pronunciation. Named as covering "г, х
   гийгүүлэгчийн хатуу зөөлний үсэг зүйн хувьд ялган тэмдэглэх, эгшиг зохицох,
   гийгүүлэгч зохицох".
2. **Үг зүйн зарчим** — hold the shape of root/stem/suffix stable regardless of
   pronunciation (айх, айдас, аюул, аянга from one root).
3. **Түүхэн уламжлалын зарчим** — traditional spellings survive pronunciation
   change (өгсөө).
4. **Бэлгэ тэмдгийн зарчим** — spell homophones apart to keep them distinct.

## ★ The дэвсгэр classification (1, item 2)

The load-bearing table. Everything in §2.1.2 keys off it.

| class | letters |
|---|---|
| **Зөөлөн дэвсгэр** | н, м, л, нг, й, в |
| **Хатуу дэвсгэр** | **б, г, р, с, д** |

A stem ending in an open syllable (a vowel) patterns with **зөөлөн** for the
same endings (2.1.3).

## ★★ §2.1.2 Гийгүүлэгч зохицох — consonant agreement

Preamble: after a stem ending in a дэвсгэр, any ending with consonant-initial
variants is written in the variant agreeing with that дэвсгэр in **хатуу/зөөлөн**
and **дуутай/дуугүй**.

### 2.1.2.1 — after a ХАТУУ дэвсгэр (б, г, р, с, д): the voiceless variants

- dative-locative **-т** (not -д) — автах, гартах
- passive **-т**
- imperfective converb **-ч** (not -ж) — авч, хярч, өгч, сурч
- unwitnessed past **-чээ** (not -жээ) — авчээ, хярчээ, өгчээ, сурчээ

The same list's контраст items **осож, дадаж; осожээ, дадажээ** are vowel-final
Classical stems (osu-, dada-), so they have no дэвсгэр and keep -ж.

### 2.1.2.2 — after a ЗӨӨЛӨН дэвсгэр (н, м, л, нг, й, в): the voiced variants

dative **-д**, passive **-д**, converb **-ж**, unwitnessed past **-жээ** — онд,
номд, гэрэлд, санд; дийлдэх, олдох; ханаж, болж; ханажээ, болжээ.

### ★★★ 2.1.2.3 — no ГА after a д/с дэвсгэр

Verbatim:

> ᠳ (-д), ᠰ (-с) дэвсгэрийн дараа дуутай ᠭ (-г) гийгүүлэгч орохгүй, харин
> дуугүй шинжээр зохицон ᠬ (-х) гийгүүлэгч орно.

Its four examples: **баясгалан, өтгөн, тосгон, сэтгэл** — all written with **ХА**
(U+182C), not ГА (U+182D), despite the Cyrillic showing г.

Extension in the same clause: a verb stem ending in a д/с дэвсгэр takes the
**causative -ха/-хэ** variant, never -га/-гэ — тусгах.

**The condition is the bichig дэвсгэр, not the Cyrillic letter.** сэтгэл is in
scope although its Cyrillic has т, because its Classical stem is `sedkil` —
дэвсгэр ᠳ. Any implementation keying off Cyrillic с/ш/т/д is using a proxy, and
the proxy is imperfect: ХА after с is also legitimate where the Cyrillic really
has х (амсхий `amusqi`, зайлсхий `ǰailasqi`, гүдэсхэн `γüdüsqen`), and nothing
in the script distinguishes those.

The rule surfaces three more times in the appendices as a split pair of table
rows — the derivational **-гана⁴** (Хавсралт 1 items 18 and 1.1.3/4) and the
**causative** (1.2.2 rows 2–3) each have one bichig form for stems ending in
д/с and another for everything else.

### 2.1.2.4 — н → НГ before г, х, н

A noun or verb stem ending in a дэвсгэр **-н**, taking a suffix that begins with
**г, х or н**, replaces that -н with **их инхлэг (нг)**: хангах, тунгаах, ханах,
нандигнах, орогнох. §3.2.2(5) applies the same law across compound proper-name
boundaries.

## §2.2 Авианы байрлалын хууль — epenthesis and elision

Two vowels never sit adjacent; a consonant needs a vowel before or after. So
attaching a suffix triggers either **эгшиг нэмэгдэх** or **авиа гээгдэх**.

Documented exceptions (2.2 Жич): loan and traditional words may open with a
consonant cluster (бар, арслан) or carry a double vowel (мантуу, түүх);
onomatopoeia and dialect (шуугих); form-distinguishing lengthening (дуу, зөрүү);
у/ө weakened to half-vowel before a soft дэвсгэр (дүү, туулай).

### 2.2.1.1 nouns

Vowel-final stem → the consonant-initial variant of the ending; consonant-final
stem → the vowel-initial variant. үнэний, алсын, ойн, үнээгийг, ойд, ойгоо.

### 2.2.1.2 verbs — the changing/non-changing letter classes

- **Хувилгагч үсэг**: the дэвсгэр letters **н, б, д, р, с, х, г**, and endings
  beginning **ү, г, н, нг, м, л, р**. `-шгүй` and `-схий` belong here too.
- A хувилгагч-final stem plus a хувилгагч-initial ending inserts the linking
  vowel **у/ү**, or the linking syllable **гу/гү**.
- After a **г**-final stem the ending joins with a vowel-revived **-гу-/-гү-**.
- A **б**-final stem plus a б-initial ending inserts one identical linking vowel.
- **Үл хувилгагч үсэг**: **б, с, д, ж, ч, т** — endings beginning with these
  attach directly to any verb stem. The causative **-га/-гэ** is non-changing.

### 2.2.2 derivational suffixes

- consonant-final **noun** stem + consonant-initial дагавар → insert **а/э**
  (зүйлс, нутагрхаг)
- consonant-final **verb** stem + same → insert **у/ү** (гарал, наадам, сурлага,
  боломж); Жич: салан, дүүрэн, салаа take а/э by convention
- **-мал⁴** → у/ү (нэгдмэл, үүсмэл, хуулмал)
- **-мхай³/-мгай³/-маг⁴** → direct after д, р, с; у/ү after н, г, б; the
  syllable гу/гү after a г-final stem

## ★ §2.3 Залгах vs дагуулах — the connector question

This is the section that decides whether a suffix follows an MVS or is fused,
and it is stated as a clean split that holds throughout both appendices:

| | written | appendix wording |
|---|---|---|
| **Нөхцөл** — case, possessive, and their stacks | **дагуулж** (detached; MVS) | Хавсралт 2 rows: "дагуулна" |
| **Дагавар** — derivational | **залгаж** (fused) | Хавсралт 1 rows: "залгана" |
| **Verb endings** | **залгаж** (fused) | 2.3.2, "үйлийн нөхцөлүүдийг цөм залгаж" |

Named cases:

- **2.3.1** genitive, comitative, plural **-нууд²/-ууд², нар**, and **-гүй** are
  дагуулж. Жич: pronoun case forms (миний, чиний, надад, намайг, чамайг) are
  written joined by convention.
- **2.3.2** plural **-д, -с, -чууд²/-чуул²** and every verb ending are залгаж.
- **2.3.3** after a genitive, **-х/-хан⁴** is дагуулж (тэдний(х), гадаадынхан);
  elsewhere залгаж (холхи, ойрх, дараах).
- **2.3.5** дагах баймж үгс are дагуулж after the host — энэхүү, тэрхүү, нэг л,
  бас ч, юм уу, хэн бэ.
- **2.3.6** **-тай³** as a case-like ending is дагуулж; as an adjective-forming
  дагавар it is **always залгаж** (хэрэгтэй, ёстой, санаатай).
- **2.3.7** **-гүй** lexicalised (эзгүй, ашгүй, бусгүй) is one word; otherwise
  separated.
- **2.3.8** **-хий** after reduplicated/mimetic words is separate (нирхийх,
  ухас ухас хийх); after с, б, г, д, нг it is attached (анивхийх, ухасхийх).

## ★ Хавсралт 2.1.1 — case endings, with their conditions

All дагуулж.

| case | condition → variant |
|---|---|
| **Харьяалах** (-ын/-ийн/-ы/-ий/-н) | consonant other than н → `-un/-ün`; **н-final and words with a тогтворгүй н** → `-u/-ü`; vowel → `-yin` |
| **Өгөх орших** (-д/-т/-аа⁴) | vowel or зөөлөн дэвсгэр → `-du/-dü`; **хатуу дэвсгэр** → `-tu/-tü`; consonant-final and тогтворгүй-н words → `-a/-e` |
| **Заах** (-ыг/-ийг/-г) | consonant → `-i`; vowel → `-yi`; gender-independent |
| **Гарах** (-аас⁴) | one form regardless |
| **Үйлдэх** (-аар⁴) | vowel → `-bar/-ber`; consonant → `-iyar/-iyer` |
| **Хамтрах** (-тай³, -лугаа²) | `-tai/-tei` regardless; plus `-luγa/-lüge` |
| **Чиглэх** (-руу²/-луу²) | **one form regardless of gender** |

**"Тогтворгүй н" is the rulebook's term for the unstable/hidden final -n** (мод →
модны). It is a first-class condition on both the genitive and the dative — not
a lexical curiosity, but part of how those two endings select their allomorph.

### Хамаатуулах and the stacks

- **Эзэнд хамаатуулах (-аа⁴)**: vowel → `-ban/-ben`; consonant → `-iyan/-iyen`
- **I/II бие** (минь, маань / чинь, тань): two forms each, stem-independent
- **III бие** (нь): a harmony pair plus an invariant form
- **Stacks** (Хавсралт 2, rows 12–17): харьяалах+эзэнд (-ынхаа²/-ийнхаа⁴/
  -ныхаа²/-нийхаа³) splits by the three genitive conditions; өгөх орших+эзэнд
  (-даа⁴/-таа⁴) splits by зөөлөн vs **хатуу дэвсгэр**; заах+эзэнд, гарах+эзэнд,
  үйлдэх+эзэнд, хамтрах+эзэнд follow their base case's condition.

## ★ Хавсралт 2.2 — verb endings, with their conditions

### 2.2.1 participles

- **-сан⁴** → `-γsan/-gsen`
- **-гч** → vowel-final direct; **consonant-final stems insert у/ү**
- **-маар⁴**, and rows 4–15 (-ууштай², -шгүй, -лтай³, -аа⁴, -аагүй⁴, -даг⁴, -х,
  -хуй², -хуун², -хуйц², -хуйя², -хнаа⁴): harmony only, stem-independent

### ★★ 2.2.2/16 the imperfective converb (Зэрэгцүүлэх, -ж/-ч)

**-ж and -ч are one ending with two variants**: the -ж form after a **vowel or
зөөлөн дэвсгэр**, the -ч form after a **хатуу дэвсгэр**. This is 2.1.2.1 stated
for this ending, and it is what disambiguates bare -ч from the agentive
noun-former **-аач⁴**, which only ever follows a vowel.

Other converbs: -тал⁴, -хлаар⁴, -хул³, -бал⁴/-вал⁴, **-ваас⁴** (б-final stems
insert у/ү), -вч, -н, -нгаа⁴, -нгуут², **-аад⁴** (vowel-final direct;
**consonant-final stems insert у/ү**), -саар⁴, -лгүй, -магц⁴, -маажин⁴, -хаар⁴,
-руун².

### ★★ 2.2.3 finite tenses

- **Мэдэгдээгүй өнгөрсөн (-ж, -жээ/-чээ)** — same хатуу/зөөлөн split as the
  converb
- **Мэдэгдэж өнгөрсөн (-бай³/-вай³/-в)** — attaches directly, **but a б-final
  verb stem inserts у/ү**. This single condition predicts авав `abuba` against
  болов `bolba`, төгсөв `teγüsbe`, сонсов `sonusba` — four attestations, no
  residue. Recorded downstream as an irreducible lexical split before this
  rulebook was read.
- **Саяхан мэдэгдэж өнгөрсөн (-лаа⁴)**
- **Одоо ба ирээдүй (-на⁴, -нам⁴, -юу², -муй², -муу²)** — vowel-final direct;
  **consonant-final verb stems insert у/ү**

### 2.2.4 personal endings

I бие: -я/-е/-ё (vowel direct, consonant у/ү), -мз, -су², -сугай². II бие:
-аарай⁴, -аач⁴, -гтун², -гдахуй², -дхуй², -дхуун², -уузай², -уужин².
III бие: -г, -аасай⁴, -тугай².

### ⚠ On reading the facing bichig column

The scan is legible, contrary to what this file said before 2026-07-31. The
page images are 1448×2048; a whole-page render downsamples far below that, which
is what made the column look hopeless. Pull the embedded image at native
resolution, rotate 90° **counter-clockwise** — the appendices set each entry as
a vertical column, so the bichig reads left-to-right once rotated — and crop one
entry at a time.

Legible is not the same as safe to copy. These are 385 short words in a script
where a stroke decides the letter, and the failure mode of reading them wrong is
silent: it produces plausible data that no test can catch. **Transcribe them
against a reader's verdict, not from the scan alone.** Two OCR-class traps found
while transcribing the Cyrillic, which is far easier than the bichig:

- 4.2 reads **биттий** at any zoom; the word is битгий. Serif т and г are one
  stroke apart at this resolution.
- 5 lists **гэнгээ** twice (24, 25) with different bichig. One of the two is
  probably a different word.
